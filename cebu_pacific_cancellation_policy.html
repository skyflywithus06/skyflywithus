Understanding Cebu Pacific Flight Cancellation Rules

Cebu Pacific generally follows a non-refundable booking policy for regular and promotional fares. This means passengers cannot automatically receive their money back simply because they decide not to travel.

However, certain situations may qualify for cancellation or refund assistance. These include:

  • Flights canceled by Cebu Pacific.
  • Significant schedule changes or qualifying flight disruptions.
  • Serious medical conditions supported by appropriate documents.
  • Death of a passenger or an immediate family member.
  • Eligible bookings with an existing CEB Flexi add-on.
  • Certain eligible flights departing from South Korea.

Passengers should review their booking conditions before requesting cancellation because eligibility depends on the reason for cancellation, route, fare conditions, and additional services purchased.

Cebu Pacific Refund Policy: When Can You Get Your Money Back?

The Cebu Pacific Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which passengers may request money back for an unused booking.

Refunds are generally available in specific situations rather than for every voluntary cancellation.

1. Airline-Initiated Flight Cancellation

If Cebu Pacific cancels your scheduled flight, passengers may choose from several available options:

  • Rebooking onto the next available flight.
  • Free rebooking within the permitted 30-day window before or after the original schedule.
  • Converting the eligible booking value into a Travel Fund.
  • Requesting a refund to the original payment method.

The available options depend on the circumstances of the disruption and the airline's applicable policies.

2. Medical Emergencies

Passengers experiencing serious medical conditions may request cancellation or refund assistance. Pregnancy-related medical restrictions and certain situations involving immediate family members may also qualify.

Supporting medical documents are generally required. Travelers should contact Cebu Pacific through its official website or app to submit their request.

3. Death or Bereavement Situations

Cebu Pacific provides assistance for eligible bereavement-related cancellation requests. These may involve the death of a booked passenger or an immediate family member.

Passengers must contact the airline's live support team and provide the necessary supporting documents for review.

4. Incorrect Booking or Personal Reasons

Changing your mind, selecting the wrong travel date, experiencing a schedule conflict, or having visa-related issues does not automatically qualify a booking for cancellation or a refund.

In these circumstances, passengers may explore rebooking options through Manage Booking, subject to the applicable charges and fare difference.

Cebu Pacific Cancellation Fees and Charges

The Cebu Pacific Cancellation Fees depend on the booking conditions and the type of cancellation requested.

For ordinary voluntary cancellations, passengers generally cannot recover their ticket value unless an eligible cancellation option applies.

Here are some important points to understand:

  • Cancellation charges: Applicable fees may be deducted when a cancellation or refund is permitted under the relevant conditions.
  • Refund processing fees: Eligible refunds may be subject to processing charges, except where the airline's disruption policy provides otherwise.
  • Fare differences: When changing to a more expensive flight, passengers may need to pay the difference.
  • Additional services: Purchased add-ons may have separate cancellation and refund conditions.
  • Government taxes: Certain eligible unused government charges may be refundable through the airline's designated process.

There is no single cancellation fee applicable to every Cebu Pacific booking. Passengers should check the amount displayed for their specific reservation before confirming a transaction.

How to Cancel a Cebu Pacific Ticket Online

Passengers with an eligible cancellation option can manage their booking through the Cebu Pacific website or mobile application.

Follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Open the official Cebu Pacific website at https://www.cebupacificair.com/ and navigate to the booking management section.

Step 2: Log In to Your Account

Sign in to your MyCebuPacific account. An account is required for booking changes and eligible cancellation requests.

Step 3: Find Your Reservation

Open Manage Booking or My Bookings and select the reservation you want to manage.

Step 4: Review Available Options

Check whether your booking offers a cancellation, refund, or other eligible option. The choices depend on your ticket conditions and reason for cancellation.

Step 5: Select the Appropriate Option

If cancellation is available, follow the instructions displayed on the screen. For refund requests, select the relevant reason and provide the requested information.

Step 6: Submit Your Request

Upload any required supporting documents and submit the request. Keep your confirmation or case reference for future communication.

Always review the final amount and conditions before submitting your request.

Cebu Pacific Ticket Cancellation Through CEB Flexi

Passengers who already purchased CEB Flexi for an eligible booking may have an additional voluntary cancellation option.

CEB Flexi allows eligible passengers to cancel their flight up to two hours before departure and convert the eligible booking value into Travel Fund.

Important conditions include:

  • A MyCebuPacific account is required to manage the booking.
  • The cancellation must be completed within the permitted time.
  • The full booking amount is not necessarily converted into Travel Fund.
  • Certain charges and add-ons are excluded from the conversion.
  • CEB Flexi is no longer available for new purchases, but existing eligible bookings can still use it under the applicable terms.

Travelers should remember that Travel Fund is a virtual wallet for future flight bookings and add-ons. It cannot be exchanged for cash.

What Happens If You Miss Your Cebu Pacific Flight?

Missing a scheduled departure without completing the required changes can result in a no-show.

According to the airline's guidelines, passengers who do not board their scheduled flight may lose the fare and other applicable fees. The booking may be considered flown.

To avoid this situation, contact the airline before departure if you know that you cannot travel. Check whether rebooking or another eligible option is available.

Cebu Pacific Refund Rules for Delayed or Disrupted Flights

Passengers affected by airline-related disruptions may receive additional flexibility under the applicable passenger rights and airline policies.

For example, a flight canceled by Cebu Pacific may qualify for rebooking, Travel Fund conversion, or a refund.

For schedule changes of one hour or longer, the airline's published passenger-rights policy provides options that may include free rebooking within the permitted 30-day window or Travel Fund conversion.

For qualifying long delays, passengers may also have additional choices depending on the circumstances and applicable conditions.

Keep your booking confirmation and any disruption notifications because these details can help when submitting a request.

How Long Does a Cebu Pacific Refund Take?

Refund processing time depends on the reason for the request, payment method, and supporting documentation.

Cebu Pacific states that refunds for qualifying disrupted flights are processed within 15 days in the original form of payment. However, the time required for the amount to appear in your account may vary.

Depending on your payment provider, the credit may take up to two billing cycles.

Passengers can check their refund status through the airline's website or mobile application. If a request remains unresolved for more than 15 days, they can submit a Guest Feedback Form for further assistance.
