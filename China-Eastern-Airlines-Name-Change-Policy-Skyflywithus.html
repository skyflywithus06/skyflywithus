China Eastern Airlines Name Correction Policy

Name corrections on tickets at China Eastern Airlines may be available to passengers who meet the airline's eligibility criteria. The conditions for correcting ticket details are determined by the airline's applicable policies regarding passenger name errors.

Such instances could be:

  • Wrong spellings of names written in English language or pinyin.
  • Switching the position of the first and last names.
  • Missing or repeated names.
  • Differences in regard to middle names.
  • Some variations related to previous or married names accompanied by proof.
  • Difference in simplified and traditional Chinese.

The process of making corrections is dependent upon verification by the airline.

China Eastern Airlines Name Change Fees

The fee that will be charged for amending passenger details will depend on the situation and specific terms for the tickets involved. Passengers need to know the difference between an amendment of a spelling mistake and that of a total passenger name change.

Although China Eastern has set out procedures for amending tickets which can be amended, no universal fee is set by the carrier in respect of all name-related amendments.

Before asking for the correction of passenger information, bear the following in mind:

China Eastern Airlines Legal Name Change Policy

If passengers have changed their name due to marriage, divorce, or any other legal reasons, additional proof may be required to back up their request.

Some of the documents that could be presented as proof are:

  1. Passport and government ID with the new name.
  2. Marriage certificate.
  3. Decree of divorce.
  4. Certificate of legal name change.
  5. Any other official documents showing the connection between the old and new names.

These documents will be reviewed by China Eastern Airlines prior to determining whether the request for corrections will be approved or denied.

It is important to distinguish legal name change from transferring a ticket. Tickets cannot be transferred to another person.
